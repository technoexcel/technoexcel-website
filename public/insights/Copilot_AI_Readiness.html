Sep 2025

The Promise - "AI for everyone"

v
Microsoft launched free Copilot Chat inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneNote for all M365 commercial users. No license upgrade needed. Organisations built workflows, trained staff, and embedded AI into daily work - all assuming it was a permanent M365 feature.
Mar 17 '26

The Small Print - buried in admin notices

v
Microsoft quietly sent MC1253858 and MC1253863 to large tenants (2,000+ seats). Not a blog post. Not a support page. Most IT teams nearly missed them. Nine working days to prepare - for a change affecting thousands of employees.
Apr 15 '26

The Wall - Copilot disappears from Office apps

v
In-app Copilot removed from Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneNote for enterprise orgs. The only official path: $30/user/month for M365 Copilot Premium. For 500 people that's $180,000 a year - before training or governance costs.
